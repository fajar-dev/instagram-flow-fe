## Prompt final

Use case: stylized-concept. A panoramic 3:1 dashboard welcome banner background for AMTI engineering Instagram content workspace. Premium soft claymorphism with subtle frosted glass, warm ivory lavender mint and peach. Composition is critical: left 50 percent is mostly empty pale lavender space for HTML heading, rightmost 22 percent is quiet pastel space for an HTML metric card; the illustrative subject is small and entirely contained between 52 and 76 percent of the canvas width. Subject: a miniature creative publishing desk sculpture with two upright social post tiles showing a cable-stayed bridge with mint structural sensor dots and a building, a lavender paper-plane publish symbol and a peach heart. No camera. Delicate faint architectural arcs and gentle curved layered paper terrain across background, very low contrast in text areas. Soft diffuse studio light, matte tactile 3D forms, polished professional editorial composition, pleasant but restrained. No text, no letters, no logos, no watermark, no interface screenshot, no border. Full bleed wide artwork.
